Two-sided marketplace · Founder & sole engineer

CleanrConnect

An on-demand cleaning marketplace for iOS, Android and web. Property owners post a job, background-checked cleaners request it, and payment sits in escrow until the owner approves photo-verified work.

  • Escrow and payouts with Stripe Checkout, manual-capture deposit holds and Stripe Connect transfers to cleaners.
  • Trust and safety: Checkr background checks, before/after photos per task, and fuzzed job locations until a cleaner is booked.
  • A full job lifecycle as a state machine, with booking deadlines, cancellation and forfeit rules enforced by scheduled jobs in Postgres.
  • One source of truth for money math, shared by the mobile app and the server, so fees and refunds can't drift apart.
  • Expo / React Native

Research harness · Personal R&D

AutoInvest

A disciplined experiment to test whether a five-model AI ensemble has any real predictive edge in markets, for about $6 a month, before a single dollar is at risk.

  • Pre-committed success criteria and a 90-day no-tweaking rule, so the result can't be rationalised after the fact.
  • Four parallel arms (AI, benchmark, random and always-buy) plus confidence calibration checks.
  • Advisory only by construction: no trading client exists in the codebase, and a deterministic risk gate sits between the models and the ledger.

  2. 02

    Written scope
